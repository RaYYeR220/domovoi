Domovoi for Meta Quest 3

It only moves things when you’re not looking.

A house spirit lives in your real room and rearranges things in your blind spots. Catch each change by framing it with your hands.

How it plays

Mixed reality · hands only · seated · 7 minutes

0 moved in your blind spot · 0 noticedMove your pointer to turn your head.
  1. 01

    Look away

    The domovoi only acts in your blind spot: outside your view, or behind your real furniture.

  2. 02

    Look back

    Something is different. A cup has turned, a candle has lit itself, the clock says 3:33.

  3. 03

    Frame it

    Make a frame with both hands. The spirit photograph pins itself to the board on your wall.

Seated, and never behind you. Everything happens in the 200° in front of your chair. A new night every day; the spirit grows bolder and leaves small gifts.

Accessible. Eyes-only mode: hold your gaze on a change to take the photo, no hands needed. Every sound cue is captioned with the direction it came from.
